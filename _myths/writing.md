Most languages that have ever existed were never written down. Children learn to speak or sign without any instruction, but they have to be taught to read.

Spelling often preserves older pronunciations or borrowed conventions, so when spelling and speech disagree, it's usually spelling that is out of date.

## Caveats

- **Writing feeds back into speech.** Standard languages are shaped by their written forms, and spelling can change pronunciation, as with the restored *t* some people say in *often*.

## Examples

- **Knight** keeps a *k* and a *gh* that were once pronounced.
- **Sign languages** are full natural languages, and most have no widely used written form.
